UNDERSTANDING THE REPORT

How is happiness measured?

The World Happiness Report measures how people evaluate their lives rather than simply adding economic or social indicators together.

01

The Cantril Ladder

People are asked to imagine a ladder from 0 to 10. The bottom represents the worst possible life for them, while the top represents the best possible life.

10 Best possible life
5 Middle
0 Worst possible life
02

Life Evaluation Score

0 — 10

A higher score means people, on average, report a better evaluation of their lives.

03

How countries are ranked

The ranking is based on average life evaluations. The 2026 ranking uses a three-year average covering 2023–2025.
