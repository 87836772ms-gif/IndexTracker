HOW IT IS MEASURED

The Cantril Ladder

World Happiness Report ↗
10 · Best possible life
0 · Worst possible life

People evaluate their own lives on a 0–10 scale.

The World Happiness Report asks respondents to imagine a ladder from 0 to 10. The bottom represents the worst possible life for them; the top represents the best possible life. Countries are ranked by the average of these self-assessments.

Important: the six factors below do not get added together to create the ranking. They are used to explain differences in life evaluations.

02Social support

04Freedom to make life choices
